# The 200 — icons to animate, most used first

The catalogue for `icon-motion.md`: the two hundred apps a Golem owner is most
likely to put on the dock, in my estimate of global desktop + web use (no live
data; the top is the world's, the middle is the Linux desktop's, the tail is
the long one). Every one of the 71 webapps in `~/.config/webapps.list` is here.

- **Dock id**: a webapp is `webapp-<slug>`; a native app its `.desktop` id (the
  usual one — check the installed file). A `+` means the webapp is not in the
  catalog yet and gets added with it. Two ids on one row share the mark, so one
  motion serves both (the native Spotify and the webapp are the same icon).
- **Mark**: `si:<slug>` = simple-icons' path (one colour: fill it with the
  brand's); `official` = the brand's own multi-colour geometry is needed (the
  Google family, Microsoft, Figma, Slack…); `app` = the program's own icon from
  its package.
- **Status**: ✅ on the dock · 🧪 in the lab · blank = to do.

## How this list is worked

Per `icon-motion.md` §9: a batch is a slice of this table (say #5–#20). First
the **motion cards** for the slice, in one table, for Max to strike or edit;
then the approved cards into the lab, three plates each; then his rounds; then
the dock. Rows sharing a mark are done once. When a `+` row is built, its line
goes into the webapp catalog too (`Name | URL | icon` — and the dock reads
`~/.config/webapps.list`, not Golem's copy, until that is fixed).

Done: 9 of 200 (10 with the compass). Lab: 1.
