# The Colours model

Confirmed by Max on 2026-10-20 in the mockup (https://claude.ai/artifact/AFYi6jHXHHT8d79r3T2Fgb). This is THE spec for the Colours box and its engine; the code follows it.

**The box:** cards first (palettes 7×5 — ⚠ ~1450 px tall at full size, does NOT fit Max's 1250 px screen; decide 7×4 / smaller cards / scroll), page dots, then tabs **Backgrounds · Colours** (centred, Colours opens), then **Auto** (centred when on; slides to the left of the section tabs when off), then section tabs **Shell · Apps · Icons · Web** (Shell first: the box itself wears it) + one description line, then the sliders. Backgrounds has the same shape for the wallpaper. Under Auto nothing below the switch is shown.

**Per tab (Colours, Backgrounds), independent:** AUTO = Golem's defaults (Colour as made, Saturation as made, Icons' Colour 0, the palette wallpaper whole, a picture as it is) + Ambient on; Auto off = Ambient off and the sliders frozen where Ambient had them (nothing jumps). Auto is the FACTORY state → Golem out of the box is light by day, dark by night (identity decision, Max confirmed).

**Three sliders per surface (Shell, Apps, Icons, Web, wallpaper):** LIGHTING = one line, left half Dark, right half Light, an 18 px gap in the middle that the knob leaps across (0..45 Dark, 55..100 Light, 50 of each half = as designed; readout "Dark n / Light n"); crossing commits on RELEASE in the real dock. COLOUR (UI word: PALETTE) = how much the palette tints the grounds; shown "0%", never "Off", and for Apps/Icons/Web 0% IS the makers' own colours (Max's original intent, settled 2026-10-20 evening — the 0→1% step is imperceptible for most apps). SATURATION = greyscale ↔ as made ↔ oversaturated (new readability axis for the floors — the_pairs must cover it). Icons' Lighting has only two real positions (two icon tones).

**AMBIENT** = that tab's light follows the sun over the WHOLE line: Light by day (to ~Light 60 midday), Dark after dusk (Dark 60 → Dark 20 by night), like macOS Auto; its switch sits at the end of the Lighting row while off and glides up beside Auto while on; while on the whole Lighting line is hidden. Smoothness, decided: (1) everything Golem paints (shell, dock, boxes, wallpaper, Files, Seam bar + pages via a snapshot dissolve) moves in tiny steps all day and CROSSFADES through the flip — never a jump in constant use; (2) third-party apps recolour in one frame → flip them when something is already moving (workspace swipe, a window opening, the overview, the user returning), cap 30 min → the 2 s screen dissolve (the scale-change one) 2 s after a touch; GTK4 apps lag until restart, accepted; (3) returning user: 2 s after the first touch, the whole catch-up over 20 s = THE noticeable dim (§7). Rejected: theme-morphing apps through mid-grey (mud).

**Apps** (the word on its own line under the Apps section's sliders; visible with Ambient on/off, hidden under Auto): the EXCEPTIONS only — the apps as tiles with their real icons where the cards are (no "All apps": the section is all apps), the chosen one's line below: `Colours ●  Dark · Light · Follow` (Colours switch on = palette, off = own; Follow = the section's look so Ambient moves it); an exception shows on its tile ("Own colours", "Dark", "Light"). UI settled: 7×4 cards (28/page), the controls span the grid's width with the three sliders side by side (Lighting · Palette · Saturation, label line + track), notches at the designed points, Ambient's two remaining sliders centred, the Apps page shrinks to its tiles, no Backgrounds·Colours tabs there. Per-app is feasible: GTK via GTK_THEME at launch, Qt via env, KDE via <app>rc, VS Code/JetBrains own files; apps not launched by the dock miss the env ones. Per-app 3 sliders REJECTED (multiplies theme files; Qt/KDE can't).

**Wallpaper:** the palette's own ("Follows your colours") has both grounds → Colour = how much the capsules show over the ground (0 = flat ground); a picture: as it is at Light 50, veiled through the Dark half, Colour = the palette grade (existing wall_tint), Saturation live; changing kind (palette↔picture) resets Colour to that kind's default. "Include the background" is gone: Backgrounds' own Ambient is that choice.

**Open for the UI round (Max's order TBD):** words (Colour→Palette?, Saturation ends Grey…Vivid, Lighting readout centred on 0), the narrow 712 px control block under the 1700 px grid, the height. Migration of today's settings (apps_dark + lighting + lighting_veil + wallpaper_tint) → per-section light/colour/sat.
